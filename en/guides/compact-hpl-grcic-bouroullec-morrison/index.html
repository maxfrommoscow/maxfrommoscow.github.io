Grcic, the Bouroullecs and Jasper Morrison: compact HPL in today's design tables

Some of the best-known designers working today put their tables on a top of solid compact laminate. Konstantin Grcic's tables for Plank and Vitra's tables by the Bouroullec brothers and Jasper Morrison all offer a self-supporting HPL top 10 or 12 mm thick, made to go outdoors.

6 min read·Updated Oct 21, 2026

In short

  • Leading designers choose solid compact laminate when a table has to go outdoors: Konstantin Grcic's tables for Plank and Vitra's tables by the Bouroullecs and Jasper Morrison have self-supporting HPL tops 10 or 12 mm thick.
  • Compact needs no board underneath and has the same face on both sides; Vitra lists it as 'HPL solid core'.
  • For permanent outdoor use, specify an exterior grade to EN 438-6: Rushil's KLADS kept a grey-scale rating of 4–5 after 3 000 hours of artificial weathering, against a required 3.

How this review was put together

This article draws on the product and designer pages of Plank, the Italian maker of Konstantin Grcic's Monza and Miura tables; on Konstantin Grcic Design's own project pages; and on Vitra's pages for tables by Ronan and Erwan Bouroullec, Jasper Morrison and Charles and Ray Eames. Each point is attributed, and the sources are listed at the end.

The designers

Konstantin Grcic trained as a cabinetmaker before studying design at the Royal College of Art. His products have won the Compasso d'Oro in 2001, 2011 and 2016, Plank notes, and are in the permanent collections of MoMA in New York and the Centre Pompidou in Paris. Ronan and Erwan Bouroullec have worked with Vitra since 2000, Jasper Morrison since 1989; Vitra describes Morrison's 'super normal' approach as breathing new life into proven solutions rather than seeking extravagant ones.

Konstantin Grcic: HPL, indoors and out

Grcic's Monza table for Plank has an aluminium frame and a top in white or black high-pressure laminate, 10 mm thick. Plank rates it for indoor and outdoor use, in the version with powder-coated legs.

The three-legged Miura table was designed to go with Grcic's Miura bar stool, which is in the permanent collection of MoMA; its top folds up so the tables can be stacked. Plank makes it with a 12 mm 'HPL full colour' top in black or white, again for indoor and outdoor use.

The Bouroullecs: 'HPL solid core (outdoor)'

Vitra modelled the Bouroullecs' Belleville Table of 2015 on the classic bistro table and offers it with tops in solid wood, veneer, melamine or HPL. For outdoors it pairs a powder-coated base with a 12 mm solid-core top, listed among the colours as 'HPL solid core (outdoor)'; the black version has an anti-fingerprint coating. Because tops and chair shells match, Vitra calls the tables and Belleville chairs 'a hybrid family of furnishings whose materiality resists classification'.

Their earlier Bistro Table of 2009/2010, conceived with the Softshell Chair, uses the same 12 mm solid-core top, also suited for outdoor use. On Vitra's German pages the material is called Vollkernmaterial — the usual German name for compact laminate panels.

Jasper Morrison: a table for museums and restaurants

Morrison's Super Fold Table of 2015 answers a specific brief, Vitra explains: museums, galleries, restaurants and similar institutions need tables that work in many settings and can be stowed in little space. One movement of the hand tilts the top upright and turns the legs so the table folds to a single plane a few centimetres deep.

Among its tops is a 12 mm solid-core version in white or black with a black edge, the black one again with an anti-fingerprint coating. That version can be used outdoors on a temporary basis.

And the Eameses, outdoors

Vitra offers the Eames Contract Tables with solid-core tops as well, and says those are the ones suited for outdoor use.

What the choice says — and where the limits are

Across both makers, compact HPL is the top chosen when a table has to leave the dining room: a self-supporting sheet 10 or 12 mm thick that needs no board underneath, with the same face on both sides.

The makers are also clear about the limits of a table outdoors. Vitra rates the Super Fold Table's solid-core version for temporary outdoor use and advises against prolonged rain, snow or direct sunlight; the Belleville outdoor version is for temporary or seasonal use, to be cleaned and stored dry after long exposure. Furniture that stays outside all year calls for an exterior grade.

Reading this against the Rushil range

Rushil's interior compact is classified to EN 438-4; the TOPAZ and VAULT data sheets report a flexural strength of 136.1 MPa against a required 80 and a density of 1.47 g/cm³. TOPAZ also shows seven core colours, so a compact top need not have a black core. For tables that stay outdoors, ask for the exterior grade to EN 438-6: KLADS, documented from 6 to 12 mm, was rated 4–5 on the grey scale after 3,000 hours of artificial weathering, against a required 3.

Common questions

Which well-known designers use compact HPL for table tops?+−

Among others: Konstantin Grcic, whose Monza and Miura tables for Plank have 10 and 12 mm HPL tops; Ronan and Erwan Bouroullec, whose Belleville and Bistro Tables for Vitra come with 12 mm solid-core tops; and Jasper Morrison, whose Super Fold Table for Vitra has a 12 mm solid-core option.

Is 'solid core' the same as compact HPL?+−

In Vitra's usage, yes: it lists the Belleville outdoor top as 'HPL solid core', and its German pages call the material Vollkernmaterial, the usual German term for compact laminate.

Can a compact HPL table stay outdoors all year?+−

That depends on the grade. The makers above rate their solid-core versions for temporary or seasonal outdoor use. Panels meant for permanent exterior exposure are classified separately, to EN 438-6.
