FSC® and PEFC™ certified HPL: what Rushil's certificates cover and how to order it

HPL is mostly paper, and paper comes from forests. Since September 2025 Rushil can supply its laminates with an FSC or PEFC claim. Here is what the two certificates cover, what the claims mean, and how to order so that the claim reaches your invoice.

6 min read·Updated Oct 22, 2026

In short

  • Since September 2025 Rushil can supply its laminates with an FSC or PEFC claim — and since HPL is 50–70 % paper, forest certification applies.
  • FSC certificate SGSHK-COC-400446 covers FSC Mix and FSC Recycled laminates until 22 September 2030; PEFC certificate SGSCH-PEFC-COC-400012 runs until 29 September 2030.
  • A delivery counts as certified only when the claim appears on its invoice or delivery note, so state it in the order. Both certificates can be checked at search.fsc.org and pefc.org.

How this guide was put together

This guide is based on Rushil's two chain-of-custody certificates, both issued by SGS in September 2025; on FSC's own explanation of its labels; and on the PEFC chain-of-custody standard, PEFC ST 2002:2020. It explains what the certificates say, not the forest-management standards behind them.

Why forest certification applies to a laminate

A high-pressure laminate is built from paper: layers of kraft paper impregnated with phenolic resin under a decorative paper impregnated with melamine resin. Researchers at the Fraunhofer Institute for Wood Research put the paper share at 50 to 70 % by weight. That makes HPL a forest-based product, and the same chain-of-custody certification used for timber and paper can follow the fibre into the finished sheet.

Chain of custody is the link between a certified forest and a certified product. Each company along the way is audited on how it buys, keeps apart, records and sells certified material, and checks that its suppliers are certified too, so that a claim on the final invoice can be traced back.

What Rushil's FSC certificate covers

Certificate SGSHK-COC-400446, issued by SGS Hong Kong, certifies Rushil's laminate factory at Kalol in Gujarat against FSC's chain-of-custody standard, FSC-STD-40-004, and its trademark rules, FSC-STD-50-001. Its scope is the purchase of FSC Mix and FSC Recycled impregnated paper and FSC Recycled packaging paper, and the manufacture and sale of FSC Mix and FSC Recycled high-pressure laminates under the transfer system, in which each laminate carries the claim of the certified material that went into it. It runs from 23 September 2025 to 22 September 2030, subject to surveillance audits.

FSC explains the two labels. FSC Mix products combine FSC-certified virgin fibre, controlled virgin fibre and/or recycled material; controlled wood has been assessed as being at low risk of coming from unacceptable sources such as illegal logging. FSC Recycled products contain 100 % recycled material, and unlike general recycled claims, which need no verification, that content has been verified.

What Rushil's PEFC certificate covers

Certificate SGSCH-PEFC-COC-400012, issued by SGS in Geneva, certifies the same factory against PEFC ST 2002:2020, the chain-of-custody standard, and PEFC ST 2001:2020, the trademark rules. It covers the purchase of PEFC-certified coated mechanical papers and impregnated paper, and the manufacture and sale of PEFC-certified high-pressure laminates. It runs from 30 September 2025 to 29 September 2030.

The PEFC standard offers three ways to run a chain of custody: physical separation, percentage and credit. Rushil uses physical separation, which rests on clear identification and separation of certified and other material through every activity in the factory. The claim on the documents reads “x% PEFC certified”, stating the certified share.

How to make sure your laminate is certified

Both schemes are clear on one point: a certificate on its own does not make a delivery certified. Rushil's FSC certificate states that products are covered only when the FSC claim is clearly stated on sales and delivery documents, and the PEFC standard defines its claims as declarations made in sales and delivery documentation, giving an invoice or delivery note as examples.

In practice, ask for FSC or PEFC certified laminate when you request a quote, so that the order is placed as certified. Check that the invoice or delivery note carries the claim, FSC Mix, FSC Recycled or x% PEFC certified, with Rushil's certificate code. Confirm that the certificate is valid in the public databases, search.fsc.org for FSC and pefc.org/find-certified for PEFC. If you will sell the finished product on with a claim of your own, your company needs its own chain-of-custody certificate.

What certified HPL gives a project

Certified laminate gives a specifier a forest-based material whose fibre is traced under one of the two international forest-certification systems, documented on the invoice and checkable online. It answers tender clauses that ask for FSC or PEFC chain of custody, such as the option in the model HPL specification published by the Italian technical portal BibLus, and it sits alongside Rushil's other environmental evidence: Environmental Product Declarations for thin and compact HPL and emission test reports.

Both certificates were issued in September 2025 and run to 2030. The product groups covered are listed in the FSC and PEFC databases, so confirm the decor and grade you need when you ask for a quote; copies of the certificates are supplied on request.

Common questions

Is Rushil HPL FSC certified?+−

Rushil holds FSC chain-of-custody certificate SGSHK-COC-400446, valid to 22 September 2030, for FSC Mix and FSC Recycled high-pressure laminates, and PEFC certificate SGSCH-PEFC-COC-400012, valid to 29 September 2030. A delivery counts as certified when the claim appears on its invoice or delivery note.

What is the difference between FSC Mix and FSC Recycled?+−

FSC Mix products combine FSC-certified virgin fibre, controlled virgin fibre and/or recycled material. FSC Recycled products contain 100 % recycled material, verified as such.

How can I check a certificate?+−

FSC certificates can be checked at search.fsc.org and PEFC certificates at pefc.org/find-certified. Rushil's codes are SGSHK-COC-400446 for FSC and SGSCH-PEFC-COC-400012 for PEFC.
