ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001: what Rushil's management certificates tell a buyer

A laminate is judged by its test results, and those results are only as good as the factory that repeats them sheet after sheet. That is what management-system certificates are about. Here is what Rushil's three certificates cover, how to verify them, and what the new 2026 editions of ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 mean.

6 min read·Updated Oct 23, 2026

In short

  • Rushil's management system is certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 by Alcumus ISOQAR, with certificates valid until 8 October 2028; it has been registered since 2007.
  • The certificates prove a documented, audited system for quality, the environment and health and safety; the product itself is proved by test results, such as the EN 438-2 results on each data sheet.
  • ISO 9001:2026 brings a three-year transition: certificates to the 2015 edition stay valid until 30 September 2029.

How this guide was put together

This guide is based on Rushil's certificate of registration from Alcumus ISOQAR, re-issued in October 2025; on ISO's own pages on certification and on ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001; and on the transition requirements for ISO 9001:2026 published in September 2026 by Global ACI, the international accreditation cooperation that has taken over the roles of the IAF and ILAC.

Three standards, one system

Rushil's management system is certified to three standards. ISO 9001 sets the requirements for a quality management system, built on customer focus, the process approach, risk-based thinking and continual improvement. ISO 14001 sets the criteria for an environmental management system; ISO describes it as assurance to management, employees and external stakeholders that environmental impact is being measured and improved. ISO 45001 specifies the requirements for an occupational health and safety management system, from hazard identification and risk assessment to worker participation, emergency planning and incident investigation.

All three are certified together, under certificate numbers 6478-QMS-001, 6478-EMS-001 and 6478-OHS-001.

What Rushil's certificate covers

The certificate was issued by Alcumus ISOQAR, a certification body based in Manchester. Its scope is the manufacture and supply of high-pressure decorative laminates, paper and metal, compact laminates, kitchen doors and drawers, across four Rushil sites in Gujarat: the head office in Ahmedabad and three units in the Gandhinagar district, including the laminate factory at Kalol.

Rushil was first registered on 8 October 2007. The latest recertification audit took place from 10 to 13 September 2025, and the certificate, re-issued on 15 October 2025, runs to 8 October 2028, subject to the regular monitoring by Alcumus ISOQAR that keeps it current.

What a management certificate proves, and what it does not

A management-system certificate shows that an independent body has audited how a company plans, runs, checks and improves its work, and goes on auditing it. It is not a product certificate: what a laminate does is shown by its test results. For Rushil those are the EN 438-2 results on each data sheet, the fire classifications and the emission and food-contact reports; the ISO certificates show that the factory producing them works to a documented, audited system.

ISO itself does not certify anyone. It writes the standards; certification is carried out by external certification bodies, and ISO does not allow its logo to be used in connection with certification.

How to check a certificate

ISO's advice is to verify a certificate with the body that issued it or, for accredited certification, through IAF CertSearch, the global database that gathers data from accreditation and certification bodies. For Rushil's certificate that means Alcumus ISOQAR and the three certificate numbers above. Copies of the certificate are supplied on request.

ISO 9001:2026 and ISO 14001:2026

Both standards have new editions. ISO 9001:2026, published on 16 September 2026, keeps customer focus, the process approach, risk-based thinking and continual improvement at its heart, and strengthens leadership, quality culture and the treatment of risks and opportunities. Global ACI has set a three-year transition: from 31 March 2028 new and initial accredited certifications are issued only to the 2026 edition, and organisations certified to ISO 9001:2015 have until 30 September 2029 to move across, at a surveillance or recertification audit or in a separate transition audit. ISO 14001 has also been published in a 2026 edition, and a new edition of ISO 45001 is in preparation.

For a buyer nothing changes today. Rushil's certificates remain valid, and its next recertification, due before October 2028, falls within the transition period.

What this means for a project

Together, the three certificates show a manufacturer whose management system has been independently certified since 2007, is monitored regularly, and now covers quality, the environment and occupational health and safety. Alongside the EN 438 test reports, the Environmental Product Declarations and the FSC and PEFC chain-of-custody certificates, they give a specifier a clear documentary picture of what is supplied and how it is made.

Common questions

Is Rushil ISO 9001 certified?+−

Yes. Rushil's management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 by Alcumus ISOQAR, certificate numbers 6478-QMS-001, 6478-EMS-001 and 6478-OHS-001, valid to 8 October 2028. Rushil has been registered since 2007.

Does ISO 9001 certify the product?+−

No. It certifies the management system that makes the product. Product performance is shown by test results; for HPL, the EN 438-2 results on each data sheet.

What does ISO 9001:2026 change for existing certificates?+−

Certificates to ISO 9001:2015 remain valid during a three-year transition that ends on 30 September 2029. From 31 March 2028, new and initial accredited certifications are issued only to the 2026 edition.
