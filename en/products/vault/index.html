Compact panel system for washrooms, partitions and lockers

VAULT

VAULT is a compact laminate specified for wet and high-traffic interiors: toilet cubicles, partitions and lockers. It is manufactured in compliance with EN 438-4 and EN 13501-1, in general-purpose (CGS) and fire-retardant (CGF) grades. Emission and antimicrobial testing supports use in healthcare, education and other regulated interiors.

01

What it is built for

  • Mass increase 0.2 % after moisture exposure (EN 438-2.15)
  • > 99.99 % bacterial reduction across eight organisms including MRSA
  • Large-diameter ball impact above 1800 mm drop height
  • Formaldehyde 0.010 mg/m³ to EN 16516; full CDPH VOC panel reported

VAULT is supplied as a panel. Cubicle hardware is documented as a separate partnership and is not part of the panel specification — confirm the scope of supply for your market before specifying a complete system.
