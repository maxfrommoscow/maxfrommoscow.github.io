Exterior compact laminate for architectural cladding

KLADS

KLADS is an exterior-grade compact laminate for cladding. Décor papers with lightfastness above 5 sit under a UV protection film thicker than 50 µm, available on one or both faces depending on how much of the panel is exposed. It is classified to EN 438-6 and supplied in standard and fire-retardant grades over a brown or black phenolic core.

What it is built for

  • UV protection film over 50 µm, one or both faces
  • 3 000 h artificial weathering, grey scale 4–5
  • Brown core standard; black core available on demand
  • Décor paper lightfastness above 5
