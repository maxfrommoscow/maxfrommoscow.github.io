Care & maintenance

General guidance based on the surface properties in the Product Data Sheets. Rushil Decor has not supplied a dedicated care document — request one before issuing a maintenance specification.

  • 01

    Daily cleaning

    Non-porous surfaces need no sealing. Warm water and a neutral detergent on a soft cloth removes ordinary soiling. Dry after cleaning to avoid deposits.

  • 02

    Stubborn marks

    Stain resistance is tested in three substance groups. Confirm any solvent or disinfectant against the stain-resistance groups in the Product Data Sheet before adopting it into a cleaning regime.

  • 03

    Avoid

    Abrasive powders, scouring pads and strongly acidic or alkaline products damage the surface. Cookware is not placed directly on a worktop: dry heat resistance is tested at 160 °C under laboratory conditions.

  • 04

    Exterior surfaces

    Exterior panels are rinsed with clean water. Do not use high-pressure jets close to panel edges or joints.

Technical data is based on manufacturer documentation and applicable test reports. Product specifications may vary by grade, thickness, décor, manufacturing batch or application. Please request the latest technical documentation before final specification or installation.
